Our story

Family-run since 1978

Harbour Fish & Chips has been serving Saltford-on-Sea from the same spot on Harbour Parade since 1978, when the Marsh family first opened its doors to fishermen coming off the early boats. Three generations later, it's still a family business — same recipe for the batter, same care taken over the chips, same view of the harbour from the counter.

We keep things simple: quality potatoes, properly sourced fish, sunflower oil changed regularly, and nothing sitting around under a heat lamp. It's the kind of chippy you hope to find in a seaside town — and the kind of place regulars have been bringing their kids, and then their grandkids, to for decades.
